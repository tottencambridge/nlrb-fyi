Everyone who has served on the Board

89 people since 27 August 1935, when the first three were sworn in. The Board keeps this list itself. What is added here is the last column but one: which President was in office when each began.

Of the 89 members the Board lists, the Board records 41 Democrats, 47 Republicans and 1 Independent.

Short service is common, because a member fills out whatever is left of a seat's term rather than starting a fresh five years. The three shortest: John C. Truesdale, 38 days, 24 January 1994 to 3 March 1994; John C. Miller, 74 days, 23 December 1982 to 7 March 1983; John C. Truesdale, 95 days, 23 October 1980 to 26 January 1981.

The President column is worked out, not quoted The Board's list gives party and dates and nothing about appointment. This site matches each start date against the House of Representatives' table of who held the office when. A recess appointment, or a confirmation that lands just after an inauguration, would make it wrong. The Board's own columns are reproduced exactly.

Where this comes from

  • The Board's own Board Members Since 1935, read on 19 September 2026.
  • Who was President on a given day: the Office of the Historian, U.S. House of Representatives.
