The five seats

A seat on the Board is not five years from the day you arrive. Each of the five has its own fixed expiry date, and a member appointed to fill a seat serves out what is left of that seat's term. The Board names them after the people who held them first.

This is why the Board's composition shifts a little every year rather than all at once: the dates are staggered so one term ends each year. It is also why a member can serve only a few months. They arrived late in somebody else's term.

A fault in the Board's own chart The Board's table runs newest first, back to 27 August 1935. On the copy this site read, 3 rows sat below that 1935 row describing 2024 and 2025, and one of them claimed to run to the present while the top of the same table showed a different Board. They look like a block left behind when the chart was last updated. This site leaves them out of the pages above and records that it did, rather than deleting them quietly.
