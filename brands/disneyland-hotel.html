Disneyland Hotel

The rule for this page

This page gathers every case where the Board's own filer wrote "d/b/a Disneyland Hotel" or "a franchisee of Disneyland Hotel", or a close variant of those words, on the case name. It is not this site's own judgment that these employers share a brand; it is what the filer wrote. Whether a franchisee and the brand it names are one employer for the Board's purposes is not something this record settles.
