Named as a brand, by the Board's own filer

A franchise case often names the local operator and then says who it does business as: "X, Inc., d/b/a Burger King" or "a franchisee of Rite Aid". This page gathers cases by that second name, read directly out of the Board's own words. It never decides on its own that a franchisee is a brand. 20 cases is the floor for a page here; fewer than that is on the Board's own listing but not worth its own page. Big names is ten names Dan picked by hand; names spelled more than one way is the same words, spelled differently. This list is neither: it exists only where the Board's own filer wrote the words.
