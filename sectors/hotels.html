Hotels and casinos

The rule for this page

A case counts here if its named party contains "hotel", "casino", "resort" or "motel".

A name missing from this page is not proof of no union. Three reasons a workplace could have one anyway and still not show up here: the employer recognized the union without an election, so no petition was ever filed; the election was certified before this site's copy of the results begins (February 2010); or the employer is a public institution, outside the National Labor Relations Act entirely, and organized if at all under a state's own public-employment law.
