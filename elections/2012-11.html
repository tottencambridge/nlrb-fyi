Where this comes from

  • Every tally is from the Board's own Recent Election Results export, last read 22 September 2026. Each case number links to the Board's docket for that case.
  • This site says what the count was. It does not say who won. The Board's export has two fields that look like an outcome, and they disagree with each other, so neither is used. A count can also be overturned: objections can be filed for five business days after a tally, and an election can be run again.
  • The Board's export gives a city and a state and nothing finer. A mark sits at the centre of that city, as listed in the Census Bureau's Gazetteer, not at the workplace. Marks in the same city are nudged apart so each can be seen. The state outlines are the Bureau's too.
