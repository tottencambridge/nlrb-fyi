Who sits on the Board

This page is legal information, not legal advice. It describes what the law says. It cannot tell you what to do about your own situation.

The Board itself is 5 seats in Washington. They decide cases. They do not investigate them, and they do not decide which charges become complaints.

Sitting now: James R. Murphy (Chairman), David M. Prouty, Scott A. Mayer, James R. Macy. 1 seat is empty.

Read from the Board's own page on 19 September 2026.

A seat does not run five years from the day its holder arrives. Each of the five has its own fixed expiry date and a new member serves out what is left of it. The five seats sets out the dates.
