Big names, gathered under one page

The Board files a case under whatever name was on it that day, and one company can end up spelled hundreds of ways: McDonald's alone is 432 spellings on this site. Each page here gathers one name by a rule printed on the page, so a reader can check it. This is a short, hand-picked list, not every name in the record: every employer and union is the complete one.
