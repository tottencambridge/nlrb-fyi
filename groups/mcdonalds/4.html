McDonald's

The rule for this page

This page gathers every case whose named party contains any of "mcdonald's" or "mcdonalds". The Board does not group cases this way, and whether these are all one employer is not something this record settles.

Most filings name a franchisee, often together with McDonald's USA or McDonald's Corp. as a joint or single employer. Whether a franchisee and the corporation are one employer was contested in many of these cases; this page does not settle that question, and gathers both sides of it under one name.
