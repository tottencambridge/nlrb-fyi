Starbucks

The rule for this page

This page gathers every case whose named party contains "starbucks". The Board does not group cases this way, and whether these are all one employer is not something this record settles.

Filed against individual stores and against Starbucks Corporation alike; store names carry the Board's own address or a store number.

2,558 cases here under 65 spellings of the name: 1,460 charges and 1,098 petitions.
