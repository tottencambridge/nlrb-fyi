Amazon

The rule for this page

This page gathers every case whose named party contains "amazon". The Board does not group cases this way, and whether these are all one employer is not something this record settles.

Warehouse and fulfillment center codes (AMAZON CMH3, AMAZON KCVG AIR) are the Board's own shorthand for a specific facility, not a separate company.
