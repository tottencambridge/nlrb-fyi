United Parcel Service

The rule for this page

This page gathers every case whose named party contains any of "united parcel service", "ups freight", or "ups ground". The Board does not group cases this way, and whether these are all one employer is not something this record settles.

Many of these cases are filed by a Teamsters local, with "(UPS Freight)" or a similar note naming the employer in parentheses; the named party on the case is the union, not UPS. Those cases are still gathered here because this page is about every case that names UPS, not only the ones where UPS is the party charged.

7,290 cases here under 955 spellings of the name: 7,209 charges and 81 petitions.
