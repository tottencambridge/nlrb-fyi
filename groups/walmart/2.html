Walmart

The rule for this page

This page gathers every case whose named party contains any of "walmart", "wal-mart", or "wal mart". The Board does not group cases this way, and whether these are all one employer is not something this record settles.

The Board has spelled the name with and without a hyphen across the years; both are gathered here.
