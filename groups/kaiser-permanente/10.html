Kaiser Permanente

The rule for this page

This page gathers every case whose named party contains "kaiser permanente". The Board does not group cases this way, and whether these are all one employer is not something this record settles.

Kaiser Permanente operates many separately named medical centers and health plans; "Kaiser" alone would also reach Kaiser Aluminum and other unrelated companies, so this page requires the fuller name.

2,318 cases here under 630 spellings of the name: 2,288 charges and 30 petitions.
